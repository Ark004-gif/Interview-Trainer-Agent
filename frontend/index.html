        HR Interview Guide

        Best practices, dos & don'ts, and salary negotiation tips

        ✅ Always Do
        • Research the company thoroughly: mission, products, recent news
        • Prepare 3–5 STAR stories for behavioral questions
        • Quantify achievements (%, $, user counts, time saved)
        • Prepare 3–5 thoughtful questions for your interviewers
        • Send a thank-you email within 24 hours
        • Dress appropriately for the company culture
        • Arrive or log in early (10 min / 5 min virtual)
        • Bring copies of your resume and portfolio/GitHub link
        ❌ Never Do
        • Speak negatively about previous employers or colleagues
        • Exaggerate or fabricate experience
        • Interrupt the interviewer
        • Ask about salary/benefits in the first round (unless asked)
        • Give one-word answers — always elaborate with context
        • Check your phone during the interview
        • Ask questions easily answered by the company website
        • Show disinterest or appear unprepared
        💰 Salary Negotiation
        • Let the employer make the first offer if possible
        • Counter with a range where your target is at the lower end
        • Justify with market data (Levels.fyi, Glassdoor, LinkedIn Salary)
        • Consider the full package: equity, bonus, PTO, remote, L&D budget
        • Get the offer in writing before resigning
        • It's always acceptable to ask for 24–48 hours to review
        💻 Virtual Interview Tips
        • Test your internet, camera, and microphone beforehand
        • Use a quiet, well-lit space with a neutral background
        • Look at the camera, not the screen, when speaking
        • Keep water nearby and notes out of camera frame
        • Close unnecessary browser tabs and notifications
        📚 Improvement Tips
        Communication
        • Practice the Feynman Technique
        • Record yourself and review the playback
        • Use STAR framework for every behavioral answer
        Technical
        • Solve 2–3 LeetCode problems daily
        • Read "Designing Data-Intensive Applications"
        • Write a technical blog post monthly
        Confidence
        • Mock interviews on Pramp / interviewing.io
        • Prepare the 20 most common questions
        • Reframe nervousness as excitement
        Research
        • Set up Google Alerts for the target company
        • Follow the engineering blog and LinkedIn
        • Review recent earnings calls / press releases

        About This Project

        IBM AICTE Problem Statement No.22 — Interview Trainer Agent

        🏗️ Architecture

        • LLM: IBM Granite via watsonx.ai (IBM Cloud Lite)
        • RAG: ChromaDB vector store + sentence-transformers
        • Embeddings: all-MiniLM-L6-v2 (local, free)
        • Backend: FastAPI (Python 3.11+)
        • Frontend: Vanilla HTML/CSS/JS (zero dependencies)
        • Resume Parsing: PyPDF + python-docx + NLU heuristics

        ✨ Features

        • Tailored question generation per role & experience level
        • AI answer evaluation with scores and improvement tips
        • Model answers retrieved from knowledge base + Granite
        • 30-day personalized preparation roadmap
        • Resume parsing (PDF, DOCX, TXT) for skill extraction
        • Curated knowledge base with 50+ role-specific questions
        • HR guidelines, behavioral frameworks, salary tips
